Probe Desktop · Windows and macOS

Know which changes to your documents need a second look.

Budgets, contracts and presentations go through many hands. Probe Desktop watches the Word, Excel and PowerPoint files of your OneDrive or Google Drive folders and, when one changes, shows you the modifications that alter a number, a commitment or a calculation, next to what was there before.

Download Probe Desktop How it works

Free and open source · Runs on your computer · No account · AI optional.

Track changes shows what someone typed. It does not show that a total now stops one row short, that a formula became a frozen number, or that a sheet was hidden. Probe Desktop reads the document itself, so these changes surface even when nobody announced them.

Who it is for

For the teams whose files carry the numbers and the commitments.

Finance and controlling

Budgets, forecasts and financial models: formulas overwritten by values, ranges that no longer cover every row, broken references, results that moved, hidden rows and sheets.

Legal and procurement

Contracts and amendments: amounts, dates and notice periods changed, obligations softened from "shall" to "may", negations added, liability or payment clauses removed.

Management and sales

Board decks and proposals: figures changed on a slide or in its notes, slides deleted or hidden just before the meeting.

Compliance and audit

A record of which version was reviewed, when, and what changed since: macros, external links and embedded objects that appear in a file are flagged too.

Assistants and project offices

Many shared files, little time: one queue of documents that changed, sorted by severity, instead of opening each file to find out.

Teams sharing folders with partners

Clients or suppliers edit the files of a shared OneDrive or Google Drive folder: see what they changed, even when nobody sent a redline.

How it works

Set it once, then only look when something matters.

  1. Choose your folders

    Pick the OneDrive or Google Drive folders synchronized on your computer; Probe Desktop suggests the ones it finds.

  2. It records the reviewed version

    The first pass keeps a private copy of each document as the reference. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  3. A file changes: it compares

    In the background, Probe Desktop compares the new version with the reference and flags the risky modifications. The icon near the clock tells you when there is something to see.

  4. You review, it moves on

    Open the findings, check the before and the after, open the document if needed, then mark it as reviewed: this version becomes the new reference.

What it flags

Fixed rules you can read, not a model's opinion.

Every finding comes from a rule that compares the two versions of the file. The same change always gives the same finding, and each one shows its location, its severity and the text or formula before and after.

Rules cover English and French wording. The application, its reports and the AI explanations are available in English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese and Italian. Documents written with Office for Mac use the same formats and are covered.

Your documents stay yours

Everything is computed on your computer.

Probe Desktop is not a cloud service. It reads the files your sync client already put on your disk, keeps its reference copies in your user profile and shows its window from a server that only your own session can reach.

If you want a plain-language explanation of a report, connect an Anthropic or OpenAI account, or a model hosted inside your company. Only the flagged excerpts of that document are sent, with the passages that still use a replaced name or term.

  • No account, no sign-up, no server of ours in the loop.
  • Findings are computed by fixed rules with no AI provider. An optional provider can explain them and raise extra findings, shown apart.
  • An explanation sends the file name, the findings, the changed excerpts and the passages that still use a replaced name or term, never the whole file.
  • API keys stay in the Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain.
  • Works with an OpenAI-compatible server run on premises.
  • Online-only files are not downloaded just to record a first version, unless you allow it.
Get started

Running in a minute.

  1. Download and open

    Get the Windows executable or the macOS application. It starts in the background: look for its icon near the clock or in the menu bar.

  2. Add a folder

    The window proposes the OneDrive and Google Drive folders it found. One click and the first pass records every document.

  3. Keep working

    When a colleague saves a change, the document appears under To review. Turn on Start at login from the icon's menu to keep watching after a restart.

Good to know

  • Watches synchronized folders. Reading the version history of OneDrive, SharePoint and Google Drive directly is on the way.
  • Reads Word, Excel and PowerPoint formats. Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote files and legacy .doc / .xls files are not read yet.
  • A finding is a reason to look, not a proven mistake; a document without findings still lists all its changes.
  • The first releases are not signed: Windows and macOS ask for a confirmation on first launch, as explained on the download page.
